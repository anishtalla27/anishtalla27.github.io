About me

I started classical guitar years before I wrote any software, and later taught myself jazz. I have played sets at local coffee shops that I booked myself.

Classical guitar taught me to pay attention to structure, and jazz got me used to choosing between different ways to play a passage. I think about similar choices in my projects. A valid guitar fingering can still feel uncomfortable. A model can improve on average while doing worse on one type of object, and an app can look finished while its data sync is still unreliable.

I plan to study computer science and computer engineering. I am in the engineering pathway at the Academy of Engineering & Technology (AET), where my interests include machines that use sensors to respond to the physical world in real time. I also want to understand how hardware limits the efficiency of AI.

  • Seahawks fan since 2018, loved Russell Wilson, still believe in him to this day
  • "Practice makes permanent," as my guitar teacher said
