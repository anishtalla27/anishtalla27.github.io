10About

Guitar, and problems with more than one right answer

I started classical guitar years before I wrote any software, and later taught myself jazz. In ninth grade I was selected by audition for the PAVAN Regional Governor's School for the Arts, and I have played sets at local coffee shops that I booked myself.

Classical playing taught me structure. Jazz taught me that a passage can have several defensible answers and you still have to pick one. That idea runs through my work: a fingering that is valid but uncomfortable, a model whose average improves while one object type gets worse, an app that looks finished while its sync is unreliable.

I plan to study computer science and computer engineering, and I am in the engineering pathway at the Academy of Engineering & Technology (AET). I am most interested in intelligent machinery, systems that sense the physical world and act on it in real time, and in the hardware limits that decide how efficient AI can be.
